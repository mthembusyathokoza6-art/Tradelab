Simulated activity only

TradeLab is intended as a simulated trading education and performance-assessment platform. No customer investment funds are held or traded by the platform, and simulated orders are not sent to a live broker or exchange. Balances shown in a challenge are virtual, have no cash value and are not a deposit or investment.

Trading and market risk

Trading financial instruments in real markets can involve substantial risk, including rapid losses. Leverage can magnify gains and losses. A simulated environment may not reproduce liquidity, slippage, spreads, financing charges, execution delays, market impact or the emotional and operational conditions of live trading. A simulated result is not a prediction of, or guarantee about, future live performance.

Market charts may use third-party data, which can be delayed, incomplete, corrected, unavailable or subject to provider and exchange licensing terms. Do not rely on a chart on this public site as a live quote or as the sole basis for a financial decision.

Challenge rules and drawdown

The proposed rules for each virtual balance tier are a 20% profit target, a minimum of five trading days, a 5% daily loss limit and a 15% overall loss limit. The daily and overall loss limits are measured statically from the challenge starting balance. The daily limit resets at 00:00 Africa/Johannesburg time. Open/unrealised P/L counts toward equity and drawdown.

Rules, calculation details, valid trading-day definitions and any additional restrictions will be published in the final challenge terms. A challenge can be failed if a published limit or rule is breached. A chart preview does not create an account or permit trading.

Possible performance rewards

Meeting a simulated target does not guarantee acceptance into a reward programme or payment. The proposed programme makes a participant potentially eligible for a company-funded performance reward after review; the proposed participant share is 80% of eligible simulated profit. It is not investment income or a withdrawal of simulated funds. Final eligibility, review, payout, tax, identity-check and dispute rules remain pending.

No personal advice or guaranteed result

Educational material and examples are general and do not account for an individual’s objectives, finances or circumstances. TradeLab does not provide personalised investment advice. No statement should be read as a promise of profit, payout, employment or future trading success. Past or simulated performance does not guarantee future results.
