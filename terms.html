1. What the Service is

TradeLab provides access to market-data charts, general educational material and simulated trading challenges. It is not a bank, investment service, broker, fund, asset manager or cryptocurrency exchange. We do not accept investment deposits, hold money for trading, invest participant money, execute real-money trades or connect to a participant’s brokerage account. A simulated balance is virtual, has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

The Service does not provide personalised financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Any educational material is general information only. You are responsible for your own decisions outside the simulation.

3. Challenges and simulated balances

The challenge tiers have virtual starting balances of R50,000, R100,000 and R200,000. Their approved access fees are R500, R1,000 and R2,500, respectively. Each tier has a 20% profit target and a minimum of five trading days. These prices are not currently available for payment: checkout remains disabled until the payment flow, provider configuration and final legal terms are approved.

  • The maximum daily loss is 5% of the challenge’s starting balance. The daily measurement resets at 00:00 Africa/Johannesburg time.
  • The maximum overall loss is 15% of the challenge’s starting balance, measured statically from that starting balance.
  • Open/unrealised profit and loss counts toward equity and the applicable drawdown checks.
  • Challenge eligibility requires reaching the target without breaching a loss limit and completing the minimum trading days. The precise definition of a valid trading day and any other pass conditions must be confirmed in the final rules.

Before a challenge is purchased, instruments and charts may be view-only. There is no free demo-trading account in the current product scope. Simulated orders, if enabled for an eligible challenge, do not reach a broker or exchange.

4. Assessment and possible company-funded rewards

A participant who reaches the 20% target may become eligible for review under the reward programme. Subject to successful review and the final published rules, the proposed participant share is 80% of eligible simulated profit. This is a possible company-funded performance reward; it is not a withdrawal of the virtual balance or a return on an investment.

Eligibility does not guarantee approval or payment. The definition of eligible profit, review standards, prohibited conduct, documentation/KYC requirements, payout method, minimum payout, taxes and processing times remain to be confirmed in the final programme rules. No reward is available until those terms have been published and accepted.
