Payment Policy

Draft prepared 8 October 2026 · Proposed effective date: [TO BE SET AFTER REVIEW]

DRAFT — NO LIVE PAYMENTS ARE ENABLED. Base ZAR tier fees are approved, but supported payment currencies, tax treatment, refunds, chargebacks, timing, operator details and applicable law are not final. Qualified legal, finance and provider review is required before publishing or accepting an order.

1. What a payment is for

A payment, if enabled, will be for access to a simulated trading challenge or educational/performance-assessment service. It is not a trading deposit, investment, exchange balance, brokerage funding or purchase of virtual money. Challenge balances have no cash value.

2. Fees and order confirmation

The approved access fees for the R50,000, R100,000 and R200,000 virtual-balance tiers are R500, R1,000 and R2,500, respectively, in ZAR. These approved base prices are not currently available for payment. Before a live checkout is enabled, the operator must confirm and display the final amount due, including the treatment of taxes, payment-currency conversion, provider charges and any other material cost.

Checkout is not active on the current public site. Do not send money based on sample or draft content.

3. Hosted payment flow

The proposed payment integration is NowPayments. For each order, the server will create a hosted invoice or payment link and associate it with an internal order. Provider credentials stay on the server; the browser must not call the provider with a private key or create an invoice directly.

An order is marked paid only after the server validates the provider’s webhook signature, checks the expected order and amount/currency, and processes the notification idempotently. A screenshot, email, browser redirect, transaction hash or client-side status alone is not proof of payment. The final supported currencies, network confirmations, underpayments/overpayments, payment expiry and manual-review process are [TO BE CONFIRMED].

4. Receipts, disputes and refunds

Receipt/invoice format, business tax details, transaction fees, failed or pending payments, chargebacks, disputes and support response times must be completed by the operator. See the separate draft Refund Policy for the unresolved refund conditions. No inconsistent or blanket “no refunds” claim is approved by this draft.

5. Referral credit

The proposed referral reward is 5% of the net access fee actually received after payment is confirmed. It is recorded separately from the payment and challenge account. Subject to final terms, it may be withdrawn or converted to challenge credit. The net-fee calculation, treatment of taxes and provider charges, withdrawal method, minimum amount, review/KYC, timing, reversals and expiry are [TO BE CONFIRMED BEFORE ACTIVATION].

6. Support

For a payment question, contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] and include the internal order reference. Do not send passwords, full card details, secret keys or identity documents by ordinary email. Operator: [FULL LEGAL ENTITY NAME AND ADDRESS].
