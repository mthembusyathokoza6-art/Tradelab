IMPORTANT: THIS DRAFT DOES NOT STATE A FINAL REFUND RULE. The operator has not confirmed refund eligibility, time limits, cancellation rights, fee deductions or payment-processor costs. Checkout must remain disabled until this policy is completed and reviewed by qualified South African legal/compliance counsel.

1. Scope

This proposed policy concerns a fee paid for access to a simulated trading challenge or educational/performance-assessment service. A fee is not a trading deposit, investment or purchase of the virtual account balance. This policy is not a statement that a refund is or is not available in any particular case.

2. Conditions requiring an operator decision

Before launch, the operator and its legal adviser must state clearly, consistently with mandatory consumer law:

  • whether and when a customer may cancel before or after access is provided;
  • how service outages, duplicate payments, incorrect amounts, unavailable challenge access or provider errors are handled;
  • how a customer requests a refund and what information is needed;
  • the decision time, refund method and expected processing time;
  • whether any fees may lawfully be deducted and how crypto exchange-rate changes, network charges or provider fees are handled;
  • how a refund affects challenge access, a payment status, or referral credit; and
  • how statutory cancellation rights, complaints and payment-provider disputes are handled.

All of the above are [TO BE COMPLETED BEFORE PUBLICATION]. Do not assume that a payment is non-refundable merely because the service is digital or access has started.

3. How to contact support

When the live service is available, customers may contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] with their name, registered email and internal order reference. Do not email passwords, payment credentials, provider secret keys or full identity documents. Final support hours and complaint escalation contact are [TO BE CONFIRMED].

4. Payment confirmation

If a hosted payment provider is used, a refund request will be linked to the internal order and verified payment record. A browser redirect or screenshot is not proof that a payment settled. The live payment process must explain pending, underpaid, overpaid, duplicate and failed transactions in the approved Payment Policy.
