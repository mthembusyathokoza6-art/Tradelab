This draft is not legal advice and is not a substitute for a POPIA assessment.

1. Responsible party

The proposed responsible party is [FULL LEGAL ENTITY NAME AND REGISTRATION NUMBER], of [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. Privacy contact / Information Officer: [NAME AND CONTACT DETAILS]. Jurisdiction and the final complaints channel must be confirmed before launch.

2. Information the planned service may process

Depending on the final features and settings, the service may process account details such as name, email address, phone number, country and date of birth; authentication records such as a password hash and secure-session metadata; challenge, simulated trade and support records; and payment/order references. The currently served preview pages do not create accounts or store submitted form data.

Identity documents, identity numbers, residential addresses or verification outcomes should only be collected if a lawful, documented KYC or reward-review purpose is approved and appropriate safeguards, retention limits and processor terms are in place. Those features are not enabled on the current public site.

3. Purposes and lawful basis

Possible purposes include providing and securing an account, operating simulated challenges, handling a payment order, responding to support requests, maintaining security/audit records and complying with legal obligations. For each purpose, the operator must confirm the lawful basis, whether information is required or optional, the consequences of not providing it, and any direct-marketing choice. These details are [TO BE COMPLETED].

4. Providers and disclosures

Planned infrastructure may include Supabase for database/authentication services, Netlify for web hosting/server functions, Twelve Data for market data and NowPayments for hosted payment processing. Only the minimum information needed should be disclosed to each provider. The operator must confirm provider contracts, data locations, subprocessors, cross-border transfers and whether any additional analytics or support vendors are used.

Private API keys, service-role keys, payment secrets and webhook secrets must remain in server-side secret storage and must never appear in browser code or this notice.

5. Security and retention

Planned safeguards include restricted server-side database access, row-level security, password hashing, secure HttpOnly session cookies, access controls and audit logging. No security measure is absolute. The operator must set retention periods for account, payment, challenge, verification, support and security records, and securely delete or de-identify information when no longer lawfully needed. Exact periods are [TO BE CONFIRMED].

6. Individual rights and complaints

Subject to applicable law and verification of identity, individuals may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability or withdrawal of consent. The exact rights, exceptions, response times and complaint route must be confirmed by counsel. Contact [PRIVACY EMAIL / INFORMATION OFFICER]. South African Information Regulator details and procedures should be inserted after legal review.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The planned login uses a server-managed secure, HttpOnly cookie. The application should not store passwords, access tokens, account balances, trades, payments or referral state in local storage or session storage. A service worker may cache static interface assets for offline display, but must not cache authenticated or business API responses. Any optional analytics, advertising or preference cookies must be identified and addressed before launch.

8. Updates and contact

The final notice must explain how material updates are communicated and identify its effective date. Contact: [SUPPORT / PRIVACY EMAIL]; operator: [FULL LEGAL ENTITY NAME].

Owner confirmation required: decide whether to publish a separate Privacy Policy; confirm the operator, Information Officer, purposes, required form fields, provider data locations, retention schedule, individual-rights workflow and any cookies/analytics.
